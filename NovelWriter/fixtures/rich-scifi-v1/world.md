# Pan-Pacific Ledger Conduit

Physical-data hybrid infrastructure: ~14,000 LEO cubesats coated in retroreflective metamaterial plus ground ceramic sensor posts every ~400m along ports, rail, and highways. Every object and many human actions are registered, timestamped, and monetized. Themes: systemic trust, engineered obsolescence, price of perfect recall.

## Factions
- Conduit Consensus Triad (three competing algorithms)
- Municipal Traffic AI guilds
- Lofoten data haven brokers
- Harbor chaplaincy networks (unlogged rituals)
- Mesh ops (cubesat coats)

## Locations
- Bitung parasol docks
- Philippine Sea freighter lanes
- Seattle Memory Market
- Lofoten black-market haven
- Pan-Pacific bridge corridors

## Motifs
- night sky as moving barcode (retroreflective cubesats)
- heartbeat as priced telemetry
- twelve milliseconds as civilizational crack
- negotiated opacity vs total recall
- wet dock planks / Bitung rain on graphene
- Faraday nostalgia (Rook's cage)

## Arc
Begin in total-recall Conduit life. A twelve-millisecond freighter latency spike exposes hidden fragility. Middle: forks, patches, and counter-patches while physical infrastructure degrades under unlogged entropy; Kwan, Rook, and Marshal Okafor collide over whether opacity is treason. End: negotiated opacity treaty — deliberate dark windows — not victory, a priced mercy. Sinta's absence becomes a permanent clause; the Unlogged Child crosses one bridge free.
