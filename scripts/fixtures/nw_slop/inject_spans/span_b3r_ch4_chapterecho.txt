=== FROM PRIOR Ch3 ===
The surface was still warm from the afternoon’s container traffic.
She pressed the key into the slot and felt the scrape of brass against ceramic, the same sound she had imagined every night since the drill that killed her sister.
“Budget lock in thirty seconds,” the guild voice announced through the overhead mesh.
“All manual interventions logged and billed at 0.7 microcredits per override attempt.”

Yen Park watched from the maintenance alcove two levels down, the dormant escrow interface glowing on his wrist display.
The credits had sat untouched for seven years, a hidden clause in the SLA he had written when heartbeat telemetry still cost 0.3 microcredits per minute.
Now the interface showed them thawing, each credit converting into active currency as the drill’s sensor posts registered genuine panic signatures from the evacuees below.
